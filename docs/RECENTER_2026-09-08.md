# Monster Recenter — 2026-09-08

## Center

Monster exists to generate a market-blind distribution over NFL Sunday realities:

1. who can play and how much;
2. how teams interact;
3. how the game evolves through one finite clock;
4. how points are scored;
5. how finite opportunities are allocated to players;
6. only after football outputs are frozen, how DFS scoring/value is derived.

The score remains upstream of DFS value.

## Validated / frozen unless new evidence reopens them

### Market blindness
Sportsbook totals/spreads, ownership, salaries and external fantasy projections have zero upstream football authority.

### League/player universe
All 32 teams and full-roster personnel are canonical. Snap participation determines player influence.

### Shared clock / opportunity supply
One game has one possession universe and one finite play budget. Independent team possession clocks are retired.

### Rushing allocation
The realized-world rushing structure is validated. Core A/B/C plus incidental rushing reproduces historical leader/breadth structure closely. Do not continue tuning rushing without new contradictory evidence.

### Receiving geometry
Conditional target-tree geometry is validated out of sample. Rank hierarchy and middle-tier structure generalize.

### Receiving eligibility
A distinct route/read eligibility state is promoted after rolling OOS validation:
- folds tested: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025
- folds passing gate: 4/4
- folds beating no-extra-eligibility baseline: 4/4
- median selected additional eligible receivers: 2

Do not flatten target shares to solve breadth. Eligibility and target entitlement remain separate states.

## Known residual weaknesses

### 1. Current health / availability reality — HIGH
Current injury evidence remains less complete than the rest of the football stack. Health must remain separate from role uncertainty and must affect both availability and effectiveness if active.

### 2. Matchup interaction — HIGH
Current player/unit evidence is richer than the interaction model. Priority interactions include:
- OL vs pass rush
- run blocking vs run front
- QB pressure response / mobility
- WR/TE archetype vs coverage
- red-zone and short-yardage matchup states

### 3. Sequential game script — HIGH
Shared finite clock and play budget are validated, but score-state feedback remains simplified. The next major game-model evolution should allow score/time state to alter pace, pass/run policy, fourth-down aggression, clock killing and comeback behavior.

### 4. Field position / special teams — MEDIUM-HIGH
Drive starts and field-position inheritance are still simplified. This directly affects scoring anatomy.

### 5. Coaching/system transition — MEDIUM-HIGH
Offseason continuity is OOS-validated as a memory controller, but coordinator/scheme changes need richer explicit treatment.

### 6. Weather/environment — MEDIUM
Infrastructure exists, but mechanism authority needs stronger historical validation.

### 7. Experimental astrology — SHADOW
Production weight remains zero. Continue only as an isolated residual experiment.

## Branch-budget rule

Before continuing to recurse on a local branch, ask:

> Is another iteration on this branch more likely to change game-score accuracy than the strongest unresolved major branch?

If no, recenter.

## Priority function

Prioritize unresolved work by approximate expected score impact:

`P(model wrong) × P(correction changes score) × magnitude of score effect`

This intentionally ranks starting QB/OL/EDGE/CB health, matchup interactions and score-state behavior above marginal WR5 allocation refinement.

## Governance

A validated layer can be reopened only when:
- new evidence materially contradicts it;
- an upstream change invalidates its assumptions; or
- downstream conservation/calibration tests expose a causal inconsistency.

Local aesthetic preference or a single current-slate projection is not sufficient reason to reopen a frozen layer.
